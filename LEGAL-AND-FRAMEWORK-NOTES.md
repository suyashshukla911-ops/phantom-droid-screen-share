# Legal / Framework Notes

Engineering orientation only; not legal advice.

## India

The product design uses specific-purpose notice, affirmative consent, data minimization, transparent scope and an easy withdrawal path as privacy-by-design objectives. The exact legal obligations depend on the real operator, data, users, infrastructure and commencement status of applicable Indian law at the time of deployment.

The project deliberately avoids claiming that an engineering demo is a legal certification.

## NIST / FISMA-oriented engineering

NIST security-control families are used as a reference vocabulary for identification, access control, auditability, communications protection, privacy, risk and incident response.

FISMA is a U.S. federal statutory framework; it is not a generic certification badge for a student portfolio.

## FedRAMP

FedRAMP is a U.S. federal cloud authorization program. This project can demonstrate control-mapping familiarity, but it cannot truthfully claim FedRAMP authorization, a JAB authorization, a 3PAO assessment, or agency approval.

## Public wording

Use wording such as:

> "Consent-first, QR-paired browser screen-mirroring lab implementing ephemeral authorization, least privilege, privacy-by-design, WebRTC signaling controls and a NIST/FedRAMP-informed security control baseline."

Avoid unsupported claims such as:

- "FedRAMP High certified"
- "FISMA approved"
- "NASA-grade secure"
- "ISRO-grade secure"
